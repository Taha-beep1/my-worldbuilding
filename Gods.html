The only ones who survived were the ones without the dark red hairs which were not that many.
And with that the first year of the calender starts and awhk's intervention resulted in him still being the most worshiped god despite of his neglects.
After Awhk saw what kartans have done he decided to no longer communicate withe the mortal realm and barely gives his blessings and gifts to anyone, But the few who do get it in our history are known to bend the very earth itself to their will.
