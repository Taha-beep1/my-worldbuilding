Zaimook

Zaimook is a dark fantacy worldbuilding project of mine based on grounded magic system
it has classes, gods, magics, nations and so many other things.
any feedback would be an amazing help!

Summory

Zaimook is the name of the planet shining by a bright golden sun with 5 heavenly realms each home to a god orbiting it with a ring going around the planet.
There are many great and small nations and tribes on this world each having their own uniqueness while still being connected to this world.
Zaimook is mostly ocieon with a single massive continent,one massive mountain range right in the middle of it and a few islands here or there.
The magic runs through each living creature on this planet and every human with enough dedication has the ability to harness that magic for different uses and join different classes.
There are uncountable different types of monsters and cursed being with many loremasters and scholors trying to document all of it yet its stil safe to live inside city walls and gaurded grounds.
